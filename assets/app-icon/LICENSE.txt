Trama app icon (trama.svg, trama-linux.svg and every file derived from them in this directory:
trama-<size>.png, trama.ico, hicolor/).

The source portrait of Charles Babbage, the photograph "Charles Babbage - 1860.jpg" (author
unknown), is in the public domain:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_Babbage_-_1860.jpg

The icon made from it (the cut-out, black-and-white portrait on Trama's purple tile) is licensed
like the rest of Trama, under either of

  * Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  * MIT license (https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

at your option.

Regenerate the derived files with `cargo xtask icon` (see README.md).
